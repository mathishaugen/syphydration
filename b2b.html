faq

common questions
from programs.

syp attaches to any bottle — including Gatorade and Powerade squeeze bottles. it doesn't replace your sponsored bottles or branding. athletes drink from whatever bottle your sponsorship requires and syp measures from the bottom.
we handle everything. devices ship to your facility. we do a 30-minute virtual onboarding with your athletic trainer or coach. athletes clip the device onto their bottle and it works from day one. no IT setup required.
the program owns the data. athletes opt in and control visibility. syp uses anonymized, aggregated data for research purposes only — never sold, never shared with third parties without explicit consent.
fall 2026 pilots run for one full season. at the end of the pilot you get a full data report, performance correlations, and a recommendation for your program. no commitment required beyond the pilot.
syp works with any bottle within our diameter range — covering owala, stanley, nalgene, and most gatorade and powerade squeeze bottles. universal fit for half-gallon jugs is in development.
syp is designed to support D1 wellness monitoring compliance. it is a passive device — no on-device coaching or AI — which keeps it within approved performance technology guidelines. we recommend reviewing with your compliance office during the pilot.
