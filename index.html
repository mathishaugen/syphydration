the problem

7 in 10 collegiate athletes start practice dehydrated. do you?

Most athletes track sleep, nutrition, and recovery. Water intake stays a guess. syp fixes that.

why it matters

potential health effects
of dehydration.

chronic dehydration is linked to higher injury risk, performance loss, and slower recovery.

Performance

Your output drops fast.

Losing just 2% of body weight in fluids measurably impairs performance. Above 5%, work capacity falls by around 30%. That's a normal training session in heat.

Prevalence

Most start already behind.

70% of collegiate athletes arrive at practice dehydrated. The problem isn't what happens during training. It's that they never caught up from the day before.

Recovery

Tomorrow pays for today.

Dehydrated athletes show higher muscle damage markers and greater soreness after exercise. The next session starts compromised before it begins.
