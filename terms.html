4. the syp device and software

4.1 What the device does

syp hardware measures changes in the mass of a bottle to estimate fluid intake and transmits that data over Bluetooth Low Energy to the App, which may sync it to our cloud services and, where applicable, to a Dashboard operated by your team or organization.

4.2 Accuracy limitations
